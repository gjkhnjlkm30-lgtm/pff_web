Ph.D. — IIT Bombay M.Tech — IIT BHU Post-Doc — Univ. of Amsterdam GATE 2001 · AIR 17

Dr. Rajesh Kumar Srivastava is a biotechnologist and bioprocess engineer with over a decade of research and teaching experience at GITAM University, Visakhapatnam (since June 2010). He completed his M.Tech from IIT BHU and his Ph.D. from IIT Bombay, followed by a post-doctoral fellowship at the University of Amsterdam, Netherlands. His research spans biofuels, microbial fermentation, metabolic engineering, and sustainable energy. He has completed two UGC-funded major research projects and has an extensive publication record across leading international and national journals.
